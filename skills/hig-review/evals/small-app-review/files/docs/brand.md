# Fernleaf brand guidelines (v3)

- **Voice:** warm, short, a little playful. "Your brew is on its way" not "Order submitted".
- **Colour:** Fern green (`colors.primary`) is our one accent. Use it for primary actions and selection only. Cream (`colors.background`) is the canvas. Every colour comes from `src/theme/tokens.ts`.
- **Type:** "Recoleta" for headlines only. Body text is the system font.
- **The Leaf Bar:** our custom bottom tab bar, with a raised centre "Order" leaf. It is our signature element and appears in all marketing. Don't replace it with a stock tab bar.
- **Motion:** gentle spring on add-to-cart. Respect Reduce Motion.
